THE ACTUAL PROBLEM

Avaya does not produce one universal CDR stream

CM sends call legs and condition codes. SM exports session XML. SBCE delivers border-session CSV files. Equinox produces conference and event XML. CDR AVAYA keeps their transport and storage reliable without pretending that their correlation models are identical.

DATA PATH

Accepted data remains recoverable

Collectors commit batches to a durable SQLite WAL spool before acknowledging them. PostgreSQL RAW rows remain pending until the source-specific processor commits the call and its details. Late CM records rebuild the complete UCID chain.

OPERATIONS

Built to be inspected

  • Original RAW records remain available.
  • Pending queues and service state are visible in diagnostics.
  • Calls open into ordered, source-linked Details.
  • Updates support rollback and SHA-256 verification.
  • Backup, restore, Support Pack and audit history are included.

DEPLOYMENT

Install online or prepare one offline bundle

wget https://github.com/vovan-T/CDR-AVAYA/releases/latest/download/install.sh
chmod +x install.sh
sudo ./install.sh

The installer deploys the application and PostgreSQL containers. The offline mode downloads a transferable image bundle and installer for an isolated server.
